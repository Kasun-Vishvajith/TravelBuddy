# TravelBuddy visual system

## Direction
A contemporary travel journal: generous photographs, calm warm-white pages, deep teal actions, and a restrained terracotta accent. Montserrat for headings and controls; Manrope for comfortable body text. Follow the user's supplied architecture and delegated design direction.

## Tokens
Canvas #f7f8f5, surface #ffffff, ink #173e3a, secondary #526661, action #0d655b, soft teal #e9f2ee, warm accent #b65b39. Cards use 14px corners. Primary controls are at least 44px tall. Content width 1240px; mobile gutters 20px.

## Composition
Explore: immersive photograph, prominent search, interests, experiences, destinations, and planning entry. Trips: Plans and Bookings views. Planner: day rail, itinerary timeline, and summary on desktop; horizontal days and vertical timeline on mobile. Details: gallery and readable sections with sticky booking controls. Guides: destination selection, labeled sample profiles, focused session configuration. Profile: useful links and actual local preferences.

## Behavior
Visible focus rings, named icon actions, move earlier/later controls alongside desktop dragging, 180–220ms transitions, and reduced-motion support. Demo status appears where users could infer a real service. Never add fabricated verification, availability, or cloud storage claims.
